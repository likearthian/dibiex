# Database brand icons

SQLite, PostgreSQL, MySQL, and Google BigQuery icons are from Simple Icons 13.0.0:

- https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@13.0.0/icons/sqlite.svg
- https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@13.0.0/icons/postgresql.svg
- https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@13.0.0/icons/mysql.svg
- https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@13.0.0/icons/googlebigquery.svg

The icon paths are unchanged. The SVG roots add `fill="currentColor"` so the
native UI can choose colors with suitable contrast in light and dark themes.

logos remain trademarks of their respective owners.
